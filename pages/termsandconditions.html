Investment Terms and Conditions

1. Investment Amount

  • The investor agrees to provide the amount specified in their contract toward Luminalia cargo operations.
  • Payments outside the contracted amount are not counted toward the investment. Please verify transfers carefully.

2. Use of Funds

  • Luminalia commits to using the investment solely for cargo running operations.

3. Profit Generation and Distribution

  • Profits from cargo runs are calculated and distributed as outlined in the signed contract.
  • The company retains a portion of profits; the investor receives the majority.
  • Insurance premiums are non-refundable but safeguard against loss.

4. Risk Acknowledgment

  • Investors acknowledge inherent risks in Star Citizen, including piracy, ship damage, or loss.
  • Luminalia is not liable for losses resulting from these risks.
  • If insurance was purchased, the investor receives their original investment back in the event of loss.

5. Reporting and Communication

  • Luminalia provides status updates upon request.
  • Investors may request additional information about their contract at any time.

6. Withdrawal and Termination

  • Investors may withdraw only when:
  • 1) Cargo has not yet been purchased.
  • 2) Cargo was lost and insurance was purchased.
  • 3) The company elects to return the investment early.

7. Confidentiality

  • Both parties agree to keep contract details confidential to avoid piracy-related losses.
  • Luminalia reserves the right to withhold exact trade routes from investors.

8. Resets and Wipes (OOC)

  • Luminalia is not liable for losses due to aUEC wipes or resets triggered by game updates.

Cruises & Tours Terms of Service

1. Booking and Payment

  • Bookings must be placed through the official Luminalia portal.
  • Full payment is required to secure a reservation.
  • UEC and other approved in-game currencies are accepted.

2. Cancellations and Refunds

  • Cancellations more than three hours before departure receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations within three hours of departure are non-refundable.
  • If Luminalia cancels a voyage, all passengers receive a full refund regardless of timing.
  • If a reschedule exceeds one hour, passengers may request a refund or keep their booking. Lack of response assumes acceptance.

3. Travel Documentation

  • Passengers must hold required identification and permits. Failure to provide documentation may result in denied boarding without refund.
  • OOC: Provide username, booking number, and class when asked for ID.

4. Baggage and Personal Belongings

  • Each passenger may bring one standard bag and one personal item.
  • Luminalia is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged belongings.
  • For safety, please change out of space suits into casual attire; storage is available for gear.

5. Code of Conduct

  • Passengers must conduct themselves respectfully. Disruptive behavior may result in removal without refund.

6. Weapons Policy

  • Weapons are prohibited unless certified by Luminalia. Unauthorized weapons may lead to denial of boarding or removal.
  • Approved weapons must be checked with security at departure and will be returned upon arrival.

7. Liability

  • Luminalia is not liable for personal injury, loss, or damage unless caused by gross negligence.
  • If first aid for tier 2 injuries or hospital escort for tier 1 injuries is not provided, an inconvenience fee will be issued and staff may receive demerits. Lack of notification releases staff from liability.

8. Changes to Itinerary

  • Luminalia may alter itineraries due to weather, mechanical issues, or security concerns.
  • Significant changes will be communicated promptly and alternate arrangements will be made when possible.

9. Privacy Policy

  • Personal information is handled per our privacy policy.
  • Data is not shared with third parties without consent unless required by law.

10. Governing Law

  • These terms are governed by the laws of the United Empire of Earth (UEE).
