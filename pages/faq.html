Frequently Asked Questions

What is Luminalia Investment Fund?

Luminalia Investment Fund is a financial organization dedicated to providing high-return investment opportunities. We also offer a membership program and curated flights and tours to give our investors the most enjoyable experience possible.

How do I invest with Luminalia Investment Fund?

Investing with us is easy! Visit our Investments page, choose your preferred contract, and follow the instructions to complete your investment. Our standard turnaround time is 1–3 days, and our support team is happy to help if you have questions.

What risks are involved?

While all investments carry some risk, Luminalia Investment Fund minimizes exposure through careful planning and expert management. We offer multiple contract durations to match your risk tolerance, with longer commitments providing higher returns. Optional insurance protects your principal from unexpected loss.

What returns can I expect?

Returns depend on your contract type and duration. We offer competitive growth, with up to 30% ROI on select plans. For detailed scenarios, explore our Investments calculator.

How do I track my investment?

After investing, contact the staff member who issued your contract for progress updates. They’ll share current earnings and estimated completion dates. You’ll also receive confirmation with transfer proof once your return is paid out.

Can I withdraw early?

Early withdrawal isn’t available because routes are planned around your investment. If, for any reason, we cannot complete your contract, you’ll receive your initial investment plus any earned profits.

How do I become a member?

Complete at least two contracts with us (as a pilot or investor), then contact a staff member to request an upgrade. You’ll receive an invoice—typically 499,999 UEC—for a one-time membership fee that quickly pays for itself through exclusive perks. Learn more on our Membership page.

How can I contact Luminalia Investment Fund?

Need assistance? Visit our Contact Us page to reach our support team. We’re ready to help with any questions.
